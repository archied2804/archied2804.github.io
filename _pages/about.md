I am currently undertaking a PhD in Aerospace Engineering, titled *Surrogate modelling for turbulent flow around a progressively refined geometry* at the University of Manchester. This work is being supervised by Alistair Revell and Alex Skillen.

I have a First Class with Honors *BSc in Mathematics and Physics* from the University of Manchester, completeing research into Mathematical Control of Dynamical Systems.

This was folloed by a Distinction *MSc in Advanced Computational Methods for Aeronautics, Flow Management and Fluid-Structure Interaction* from Imperial College London, completing a project titled, Application of Graph Neural Networks for Predicting Tansient Dynamics: Cardiac Electrophysiology.

My work focuses on the application of recent Machine Learning advancements to the domain of Aerospace Engineering, specifically the use of diffusion models in 3D non structured geometries.
